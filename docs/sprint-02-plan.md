## Scrum Master focus

1. Establish shared understanding of the Sprint Goal.
2. Help the team inspect capacity and uncertainty.
3. Encourage Developers to make the forecast.
4. Surface dependencies before work starts.
5. Ensure the Product Owner is available for value and scope clarification.

## Important distinction

The Scrum Master facilitates the conditions for a good forecast. The Scrum Master does **not** select which Developer works on which task or promise delivery on behalf of the team.
