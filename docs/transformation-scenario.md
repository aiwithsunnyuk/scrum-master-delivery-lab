# Transformation Scenario

## Situation

The organisation says it is Agile, but managers request daily individual status, stakeholders bypass the Product Owner, the Product Owner is overloaded, and teams are pressured to increase velocity.

## Scrum Master response

1. Inspect the current behaviour and its impact.
2. Make accountabilities and decision paths explicit.
3. Use delivery evidence rather than opinion to discuss the problem.
4. Coach managers toward outcome and system-level transparency.
5. Protect the Product Owner's prioritisation accountability.
6. Facilitate a working agreement for stakeholder engagement.
7. Inspect whether the new behaviour improves flow, ownership and decision quality.

## Success signal

The system needs less status chasing because transparency and accountability have become part of the operating model.
