# Escalation Matrix

| Severity | Example | Initial owner | Scrum Master action | Escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Production/security blocker threatens Sprint Goal | Appropriate technical/security owner | Immediate coordination + transparency | Immediate |
| High | External API unavailable | Dependency owner | Facilitate cross-team resolution | No meaningful progress within agreed window |
| Medium | Product decision unclear | Product Owner | Facilitate clarification | Decision threatens Sprint Goal |
| Medium | Test environment instability | Engineering/Platform owner | Coordinate investigation | Repeated disruption |
| Low | Minor workflow friction | Team | Encourage team-owned resolution | Pattern repeats |

## Escalation principles

### Escalate the impediment, not the person

The objective is to improve flow and protect the product outcome.

### Preserve transparency

Stakeholders should receive an accurate picture of impact and options.

### Avoid escalation theatre

Escalation is valuable only when it changes the system's ability to resolve the problem.

### Keep ownership explicit

Escalation does not transfer accountability to the Scrum Master.
