# Delivery Risk Review

## Risk model

Capture each material risk with:

- Description
- Probability / severity
- Impact on outcome, scope, quality or timing
- Owner
- Mitigation
- Contingency where appropriate
- Review date / trigger
- Escalation path

## Review principle

Risk management is not a spreadsheet exercise. The useful question is: **what has changed, what does it threaten, who owns the response, and when will we inspect again?**
