# Milestone 01 — Product Goal & Product Backlog

## Objective

Demonstrate the Scrum Master's practical ability to create the conditions for a transparent, understandable and value-oriented Product Backlog.

This milestone focuses on **product clarity, backlog structure, refinement readiness and Product Owner collaboration**.

## Scenario

**Product:** Customer Self-Service Portal

**Problem:** Customers frequently contact support for questions that could be answered through self-service. Service requests are also difficult to track after submission.

**Product Goal:** Create a simple self-service experience that helps customers find answers, raise requests and understand request status.

## Scrum Master responsibilities demonstrated

- Help establish a shared understanding of the Product Goal.
- Facilitate Product Backlog refinement.
- Help the Product Owner make backlog items transparent and understandable.
- Surface dependencies, assumptions and unresolved decisions.
- Encourage slicing of work into valuable, testable increments.
- Support the team without taking ownership of Product Owner decisions.
- Make risks and impediments visible early.

## Exit criteria

Milestone 01 is complete when:

- The Product Goal is clear.
- The backlog has a coherent ordering.
- High-priority stories have testable acceptance criteria.
- Dependencies are visible.
- Stories are small enough to discuss for Sprint Planning.
- The Scrum Master can explain what was facilitated versus what was decided by the Product Owner/team.
