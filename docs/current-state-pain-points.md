# Current-State Pain Points

## Observed system constraints
1. Individual status requests interrupt team flow.
2. Stakeholders can bypass the Product Owner.
3. Product ownership capacity is constrained.
4. Velocity can be treated as a performance target.
5. Cross-team dependencies surface too late.
6. Delivery governance can become reporting-heavy instead of decision-oriented.

## Diagnostic principle
Do not immediately prescribe training, tooling or additional ceremonies. First identify the behaviour, system constraint, impact and evidence.
