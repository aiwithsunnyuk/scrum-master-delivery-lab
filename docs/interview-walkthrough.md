# Interview Walkthrough

## 90-second opening

"I built this repository to demonstrate my practical Scrum Master approach. Rather than presenting only certification knowledge, I wanted to show how I would take a product from backlog clarity through Sprint Planning, delivery, impediment management, Sprint Review, metrics and retrospective improvement."

## Five stories to explain

### 1. Sprint Goal
Explain why the team commits to a valuable outcome rather than a list of tasks.

### 2. Impediment
Use `data/impediment-log.csv` to explain how you identify ownership, escalation and follow-through.

### 3. Product Owner partnership
Explain how backlog ordering and acceptance remain Product Owner responsibilities while the Scrum Master improves the system around them.

### 4. Metrics
Explain why velocity can support forecasting but should not become an individual performance score.

### 5. Continuous improvement
Use the retrospective action to show that improvement must be inspected in the following Sprint.

## Strong closing

"My differentiator is the combination of hands-on Scrum Master experience and current Product Management experience. I understand both the delivery system and the product conversation, while still keeping accountability with the appropriate Scrum accountabilities."
