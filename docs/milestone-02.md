# Milestone 02 — Sprint Execution & Adaptation

## Objective

Demonstrate practical Sprint execution: Sprint Goal creation, team forecast, daily inspection, impediment management and adaptation while protecting the Sprint Goal.

## Scenario

Sprint 02 builds the next slice of the Customer Self-Service Portal.

**Sprint Goal:** Enable customers to narrow knowledge-search results and understand when no relevant article is available.

**Forecast:** 18 story points

## Evidence

- `docs/sprint-02-plan.md`
- `docs/daily-scrum-log.md`
- `docs/mid-sprint-adaptation.md`
- `data/sprint-02-backlog.csv`
- `data/sprint-02-capacity.csv`

## Scrum Master behaviours demonstrated

- Facilitate Sprint Planning without assigning work to individuals.
- Keep the Sprint Goal visible.
- Help Developers inspect progress and adapt their plan.
- Make an impediment transparent.
- Facilitate cross-team collaboration/escalation.
- Protect focus while allowing the plan to adapt.
- Keep Product Owner, Developers and stakeholders aligned with the actual situation.

## Practical challenge

On Day 4, the article-taxonomy dependency becomes unavailable because the upstream service has a production incident.

The team does not simply wait.

The Scrum Master helps the Developers and Product Owner inspect the impact, identify options and adapt the Sprint Backlog while preserving the Sprint Goal.

## Exit criteria

- Sprint Goal is explicit.
- Capacity and forecast are transparent.
- Daily Scrum evidence exists.
- An impediment has a clear owner and follow-up.
- Sprint Backlog adaptation is documented.
- The final outcome can be explained in an interview.
