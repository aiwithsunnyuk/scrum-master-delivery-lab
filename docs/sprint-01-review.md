# Sprint 01 Review

## Sprint Goal
Enable customers to search the knowledge base and identify a relevant support article.

## Outcome

**Goal achieved.**

Completed:
- US-001 Search knowledge articles
- US-002 View article results
- US-003 Open article details

Not completed:
- US-004 Search analytics

## Demonstrated increment

The team demonstrated the complete search journey from keyword entry through article detail.

## Stakeholder feedback

- Search results need clearer relevance indicators.
- Support requested article-category filtering.
- Product wants analytics instrumentation before broader rollout.

## Product Owner decision

Analytics remains important but will be re-ordered against the newly discovered filtering requirement.

## Scrum Master observations

- The team adapted to an API dependency without losing the Sprint Goal.
- The review generated useful product feedback.
- The next refinement session should clarify analytics acceptance criteria and filtering value.

## Key lesson

A Sprint Review is an inspection of the Increment and the Product Backlog, not a status-report meeting.
