# Milestone 06 — Coaching, Stakeholder Management & Conflict

## Objective

Demonstrate senior Scrum Master capability in the human and organizational side of Agile delivery: coaching, facilitation, conflict management, stakeholder alignment and influencing without authority.

## Operating model

**Listen → Clarify → Facilitate → Coach → Align → Make commitments visible → Inspect behaviour → Follow up**

## Scenario

The Customer Self-Service Portal has reached a point where delivery is technically healthy, but collaboration is becoming strained.

Three tensions emerge:

1. A senior stakeholder wants additional scope inserted into the Sprint.
2. The Product Owner and Engineering Lead disagree about prioritization and technical risk.
3. Developers feel that recurring stakeholder requests are bypassing the Product Owner.

The Scrum Master's role is to improve the system of collaboration without becoming the decision-maker.

## Senior-level behaviours demonstrated

- Influence without formal authority.
- Facilitate difficult conversations.
- Coach rather than solve every problem personally.
- Protect the Product Goal and Sprint Goal.
- Help stakeholders work through the Product Owner.
- Surface cross-team risks and dependencies.
- Use evidence and transparency rather than escalation-by-personality.
- Follow up to determine whether behaviour actually changed.

## Exit criteria

A hiring manager should be able to see evidence that the Scrum Master can:

- Handle pressure without becoming defensive.
- Facilitate conflict without taking sides.
- Coach Product Owner and Developers appropriately.
- Create healthy stakeholder boundaries.
- Coordinate cross-team dependencies.
- Turn recurring behavioural issues into improvement actions.
