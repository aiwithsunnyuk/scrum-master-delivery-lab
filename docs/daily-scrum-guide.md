# Daily Scrum Guide

## Purpose

Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog as necessary.

## Facilitation prompts

Instead of asking each person for a manager-style status report, focus on:

1. Are we still on track toward the Sprint Goal?
2. What has changed since our last inspection?
3. Where is work blocked or aging?
4. Does the plan need adaptation?
5. Who needs to collaborate today?

## Anti-patterns to watch

- Reporting only to the Scrum Master.
- Turning the event into a 15-minute status meeting.
- Solving every technical issue inside the Daily Scrum.
- Measuring individual productivity through task counts.

## Scrum Master follow-through

If an impediment needs deeper discussion, capture it and help the appropriate people collaborate outside the Daily Scrum.
