# Sprint 04 — Retrospective

## Safety and purpose

The retrospective is a working session for improving quality and effectiveness. It is not an individual performance review.

## What went well

- Developers collaborated closely on the request flow.
- Stakeholders gave useful feedback early.
- The team responded quickly to one dependency escalation.
- Acceptance criteria improved during refinement.

## What did not go well

- Environment instability interrupted integration.
- One product decision arrived late.
- Three defects escaped acceptance.
- Too much work remained active at the same time.

## System-level observations

| Observation | Likely system factor |
|---|---|
| Integration interrupted | Environment readiness/dependency validation |
| Late product decision | Decision ownership and timing |
| Defects escaped | Acceptance/test coverage gaps |
| Work remained active | WIP and slicing |

## Improvement hypotheses

### Experiment 1
**Hypothesis:** If the team validates critical environments and dependencies before Sprint Planning, integration interruptions will reduce.

**Measure:** Number of Sprint interruptions caused by known environment/dependency issues.

### Experiment 2
**Hypothesis:** If acceptance criteria include explicit edge cases before development starts, escaped defects will decrease.

**Measure:** Escaped defects per Sprint.

### Experiment 3
**Hypothesis:** If the team limits concurrent work, median cycle time will become more stable.

**Measure:** Median cycle time and number of items simultaneously in progress.

## Retro outcome

The team selected Experiments 1 and 2 for the next Sprint.

## Scrum Master follow-up

Create visible improvement backlog items, agree how the measures will be inspected and revisit the experiments during the next retrospective.
