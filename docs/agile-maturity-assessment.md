# Agile Maturity Assessment

| Dimension | Current signal | Target state | Evidence / inspection question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product ownership | PO capacity constrained | Clear product decision path | Are priorities and trade-offs decided by the right accountable owner? |
| Team autonomy | Stakeholder and manager interruptions | Teams own Sprint execution | Can teams negotiate scope and sequencing without bypass routes? |
| Flow | WIP and dependencies create delays | Smaller batches, visible flow | Where is work waiting rather than being worked? |
| Quality | Defects can escape | Quality built into Done | What evidence shows quality is improving? |
| Leadership behaviour | Status pressure can become command-and-control | Outcome-oriented leadership | Are leaders asking for decisions and outcomes rather than individual activity reports? |
| Cross-team coordination | Dependencies require active coordination | Early dependency negotiation | Are dependency owners and dates visible before commitment? |

Use this as an inspection baseline, not an organisational maturity scorecard for individuals.
