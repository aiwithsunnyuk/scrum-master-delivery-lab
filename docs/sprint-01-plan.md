# Sprint 01 Plan

## Sprint
Sprint 01 | 2 weeks

## Sprint Goal

> Enable a customer to search the knowledge base and identify a relevant support article from the portal.

## Selected backlog

| ID | Story | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| US-001 | Search knowledge articles | 5 |
| US-002 | View article results | 3 |
| US-003 | Open article details | 3 |
| US-004 | Track search analytics | 2 |

**Planned capacity:** 13 points

## Planning conversation

The team validated:

- Sprint Goal clarity
- Story acceptance criteria
- Dependencies
- Available capacity
- Definition of Done
- Technical approach

## Risks identified before commitment

1. Search API access could delay integration.
2. Analytics event naming is not yet agreed.
3. UX copy requires Product Owner confirmation.

## Scrum Master actions

- Ensure unresolved dependencies are visible.
- Confirm the Product Owner is available for clarification.
- Avoid converting the Sprint Backlog into a command-and-control task list.
- Make the Sprint Goal the central decision filter.

## Commitment principle

The Sprint Goal is the commitment. Individual forecasted backlog items can change when the team learns more, provided the Sprint Goal remains protected and quality is maintained.
