## Final positioning

This repository is a practical portfolio simulation. It should complement, not replace, real delivery experience.

The strongest positioning is:

**Experienced delivery professional + Product Management experience + Scrum Master experience + PSM I + PSPO I + demonstrable Agile delivery thinking.**

## Exit criteria

A recruiter can understand the portfolio in under five minutes, while an interviewer can drill into individual evidence for a deeper conversation.
