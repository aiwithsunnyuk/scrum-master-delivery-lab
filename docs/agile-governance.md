# Agile Governance

## Principle

Governance should create useful transparency and decisions, not reporting theatre.

## Cadence

- Sprint-level inspection: team and Product Owner
- Cross-team dependency review: delivery representatives
- Release readiness review: product, engineering and relevant operational stakeholders
- Executive status: concise changes, risks, actions and decisions

## Minimum useful information

1. Outcome / Sprint Goal status
2. Flow and quality signals
3. Material impediments and dependencies
4. Top delivery risks
5. Decisions or support required
6. Next inspection point
