# Coaching Plan

## Coaching objective

Move the team from dependency on the Scrum Master toward greater self-management and healthier collaboration.

| Audience | Observation | Coaching approach | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developers | Stakeholder requests sometimes interrupt focus | Coach team to route priority conversations through PO | Fewer direct priority interruptions |
| Product Owner | Some decisions arrive late | Coach decision clarity and availability | Reduced decision aging |
| Engineering Lead | Technical risk sometimes appears late | Encourage early risk visibility in refinement | Risks surfaced before Sprint |
| Stakeholders | Requests sometimes bypass PO | Explain Product Owner accountability and transparent prioritization | Requests enter visible backlog |
| Whole team | Conflict is sometimes solved by escalation | Facilitate direct evidence-based conversations | More issues resolved at team level |

## Coaching style

### Ask before telling

Use questions that help people inspect their own system.

### Coach the system, not just individuals

Repeated behaviour may be caused by incentives, unclear ownership or missing transparency.

### Make progress observable

Agree on a small behavioural signal rather than a vague objective such as "communicate better."

## Follow-up

Review the success signals after two Sprints.

If the same pattern remains, inspect the system again rather than simply repeating the same coaching message.
