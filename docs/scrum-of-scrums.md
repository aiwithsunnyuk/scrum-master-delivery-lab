# Scrum of Scrums

## Purpose

Coordinate dependencies across multiple Scrum teams without creating a command-and-control status meeting.

## Participants

Representatives from participating teams, selected based on the teams' needs.

## Discussion prompts

1. What dependency do we have on another team?
2. What dependency does another team have on us?
3. What impediment is affecting multiple teams?
4. What changed that other teams need to know?
5. What needs coordination outside this forum?

## Example

**Team A:** Waiting for authentication service contract update.

**Team B:** Owns authentication API.

**Impact:** Team A cannot complete integration testing.

**Action:** Teams agree on contract clarification and test-environment validation.

**Scrum Master role:** Facilitate coordination and make the dependency visible. Do not become the owner of Team B's technical work.

## Anti-pattern

The Scrum of Scrums should not become a roll-call where representatives report yesterday/today/blockers to management.

Its purpose is cross-team coordination.
