# Metrics Interpretation Guide

## 1. Sprint Goal achievement

**Question:** Did the team achieve the Sprint Goal?

This is the primary delivery outcome in this portfolio.

## 2. Forecast vs completed

Example:

- Forecast: 18 points
- Completed: 15 points
- Spillover: 3 points

Interpretation should begin with **why**, not "the team missed by 3."

Possible causes:

- Dependency interruption
- Unplanned work
- Excessive uncertainty
- Quality rework
- Capacity change
- Overly large stories

## 3. Velocity

Velocity is an observed historical measure of completed work.

It is useful for forecasting conversations when interpreted as a trend.

It is not a target.

## 4. Capacity

Capacity explains available team time/availability.

Capacity and velocity are related but not interchangeable.

## 5. Cycle time

Cycle time helps inspect flow from work start to completion.

A rising cycle time may indicate:

- Work items are too large.
- WIP is too high.
- Dependencies are slowing flow.
- Review/testing is becoming a bottleneck.

## 6. Spillover

Spillover identifies work that crossed the Sprint boundary.

Use it to inspect system conditions, not to assign blame.

## 7. Defects

Defect data should be viewed alongside delivery progress.

A team that completes more points while defect escape rises may have a quality problem.

## 8. Practical Scrum Master conversation

Instead of:

> "Velocity fell, so what went wrong?"

Use:

> "What changed in the system, and what should we inspect before the next Sprint?"

## Important caution

A dashboard creates transparency only if the team understands what each metric means. A visually impressive chart with poor interpretation is not useful empirical evidence.
