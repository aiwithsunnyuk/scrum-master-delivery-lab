# Sprint 01 Retrospective

## Format

### What helped?
- Clear Sprint Goal
- Early API dependency discussion
- Product Owner availability during refinement

### What created friction?
- Analytics acceptance criteria were ambiguous.
- QA received one story later than ideal.
- Dependency ownership was unclear for part of the sprint.

### What should we try next?

| Improvement | Owner | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| Add analytics acceptance-criteria checklist | PO + SM | No analytics story enters sprint with unresolved event definitions |
| Dependency owner recorded during refinement | SM | 100% of sprint dependencies have an owner |
| QA pairing during refinement for complex stories | Dev + QA | Fewer late clarification defects |

## One improvement to carry forward

**Dependency ownership becomes an explicit refinement checkpoint.**

## Follow-up

The Scrum Master will inspect this improvement during Sprint 02 retrospective rather than treating the retrospective as a one-time discussion.
