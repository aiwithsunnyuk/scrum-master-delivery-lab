## Review snapshot

|---|---|---|---:|---|---|

## What happened with IMP-004?

The dependency became unavailable during Sprint 02.

The team:

1. Made the impact visible.
2. Continued independent work.
3. Escalated to the dependency owner.
4. Kept the Product Owner informed.
5. Resumed integration when the service recovered.

## Aging observation

A two-day impediment did not automatically mean two days of team inactivity. The key question was whether the team could continue valuable work while the dependency was being resolved.

## System-level improvement

**Before Sprint Planning:** verify critical external dependencies and named owners.

## Anti-pattern detected

Do not maintain an impediment register that is simply a list of complaints. Each open item should have an owner, next action and reason it matters.
