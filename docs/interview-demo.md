# Interview Demo Script

## 10-minute demonstration

### Minute 1 — Context
Explain the product problem and Product Goal.

### Minute 2 — Backlog
Open `data/product-backlog.csv` and explain ordering, value and dependencies.

### Minute 3 — Sprint Goal
Open `docs/sprint-02-plan.md`.

Explain why the Sprint Goal matters more than treating the Sprint as a fixed task contract.

### Minute 4 — Impediment
Open `docs/mid-sprint-adaptation.md`.

Explain the Day 4 dependency failure.

### Minute 5 — Metrics
Run:

```bash
python scripts/sprint-metrics.py data/sprint-metrics.csv
```

Explain why velocity is a trend, not a target.

### Minute 6 — Review
Open `docs/sprint-04-review.md`.

Explain how stakeholder feedback changes future Product Backlog ordering.

### Minute 7 — Retrospective
Open `docs/sprint-04-retrospective.md`.

Explain the difference between a symptom and a system-level cause.

### Minute 8 — Coaching
Open `docs/conflict-facilitation.md`.

Explain how you facilitate disagreement without becoming the decision-maker.

### Minute 9 — Improvement
Open `docs/improvement-backlog.md`.

Show the hypothesis/measure/owner structure.

### Minute 10 — Close

"My approach is to make the system transparent, help the team inspect reality, facilitate difficult conversations and create conditions for continuous improvement."
