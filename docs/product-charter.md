# Product Charter

## Product
Customer Self-Service Portal

## Problem
Customers frequently contact support for questions that could be answered through self-service. Service requests are also difficult to track after submission.

## Product Goal
Create a simple self-service experience that helps customers find answers, raise requests and understand request status.

## Desired outcomes

- Increase successful self-service journeys.
- Reduce avoidable support contacts.
- Improve visibility of service-request status.
- Give Product and Support teams actionable usage data.

## Initial scope

1. Knowledge search
2. Article results
3. Service-request creation
4. Request-status tracking
5. Basic analytics

## Out of scope for MVP

- Native mobile application
- AI chatbot
- Multi-language support
- Advanced personalization

## Key stakeholders

- Product Owner
- Customer Support
- Engineering
- UX
- QA
- Business sponsor
- Security / compliance

## Scrum Master focus

The Scrum Master's role in this scenario is to improve the team's delivery system:

- Create conditions for effective refinement and planning.
- Facilitate Scrum events.
- Surface impediments and dependencies.
- Protect the Sprint Goal from uncontrolled change.
- Help stakeholders understand empirical progress.
- Encourage the team to own its commitments.
- Turn retrospective insights into observable improvement.
