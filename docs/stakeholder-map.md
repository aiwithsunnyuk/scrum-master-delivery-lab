# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | Product value and ordering | High | Continuous |
| Engineering Lead | Technical quality and delivery | High | Frequent |
| Support Lead | Customer-support outcomes | Medium | Sprint Review |
| Business Sponsor | Outcomes and investment | High | Sprint Review / roadmap |
| Security | Risk and compliance | High | As needed |
| UX | Customer experience | Medium | Refinement / review |

## Scrum Master principle

Stakeholder engagement should improve transparency and feedback without creating parallel prioritization channels that bypass the Product Owner.
