# Release Readiness

## Purpose

Provide a concise evidence-based view of whether the delivery system is ready for a planned release decision.

## Readiness lenses

- Product scope and acceptance clarity
- Quality and escaped defects
- Critical dependencies
- Operational / support readiness
- Known risks and owners
- Decision authority and unresolved questions

## Scrum Master stance

The Scrum Master facilitates transparency and the decision conversation. The Scrum Master does not unilaterally declare a release ready or not ready.

## Decision pattern

**Evidence → Risk → Options → Owner → Decision → Follow-up inspection**
