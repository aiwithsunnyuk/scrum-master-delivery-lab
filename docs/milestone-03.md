# Milestone 03 — Impediment & Dependency Management

## Objective

Demonstrate a practical Scrum Master system for identifying, making transparent, escalating, tracking and learning from impediments and dependencies.

## Operating model

**Detect → Classify → Make transparent → Assign ownership → Facilitate resolution → Inspect aging → Close → Learn**

The Scrum Master facilitates removal of impediments. Ownership of technical, product or external decisions remains with the appropriate people.

## What this milestone demonstrates

- Impediment identification
- Impact and severity assessment
- Ownership and escalation
- Dependency tracking
- Aging and follow-up
- Resolution effectiveness
- Prevention through retrospective improvement

- `data/impediment-register.csv`
- `data/dependency-register.csv`

## Exit criteria

A hiring manager should be able to inspect this milestone and understand:

1. How an impediment enters the system.
2. How impact is assessed.
3. Who owns resolution.
4. When escalation happens.
5. How aging is monitored.
6. How closure is validated.
7. How repeated impediments become improvement opportunities.
