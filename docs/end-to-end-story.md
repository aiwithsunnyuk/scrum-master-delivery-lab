# End-to-End Delivery Story

## 1. Start with the problem

Customers struggle to find answers and track service requests.

## 2. Establish the Product Goal

Build a self-service experience that helps customers find answers, raise requests and understand request status.

## 3. Create transparency

The Product Backlog makes upcoming value, dependencies and uncertainty visible.

## 4. Refine

The team collaborates with the Product Owner to improve story clarity and identify dependencies.

## 5. Sprint

The team creates a Sprint Goal and forecasts work based on capacity and understanding.

## 6. Adapt

An upstream dependency becomes unavailable. The impediment is made transparent and the team adapts without losing sight of the Sprint Goal.

## 7. Inspect delivery

Metrics show that Sprint Goal achievement is more meaningful than chasing velocity.

## 8. Review

Stakeholders inspect the Increment and provide feedback that changes Product Backlog ordering.

## 9. Retrospect

The team identifies system-level causes behind missed outcomes, defects and flow problems.

## 10. Improve

Improvement experiments are added to a visible backlog with hypotheses, owners and measures.

## 11. Coach

Stakeholder pressure and team conflict are handled through facilitation, transparency and coaching rather than command-and-control.

## Final message

The portfolio demonstrates a Scrum Master who helps teams improve their delivery system while respecting Scrum accountabilities.
