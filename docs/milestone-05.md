# Milestone 05 — Sprint Review, Retrospective & Improvement

## Objective

Demonstrate how a Scrum Master turns inspection of the product and the way of working into meaningful stakeholder feedback and actionable improvement.

## Flow

**Increment → Sprint Review → Stakeholder feedback → Retrospective → Improvement hypothesis → Experiment → Follow-up**

## Scenario

Sprint 04 did not achieve its Sprint Goal. The team completed 16 of 20 forecast points, experienced two high impediments and saw three escaped defects.

The purpose is not to defend the result. The purpose is to inspect what happened, understand the system conditions and identify a small number of improvements.

## Scrum Master behaviours demonstrated

- Create a safe environment for inspection and adaptation.
- Facilitate stakeholder conversation without turning the Sprint Review into a status presentation.
- Help the team inspect causes rather than blame individuals.
- Convert retrospective observations into testable improvement experiments.
- Make improvement work visible.
- Follow up on whether an experiment produced the expected result.

## Exit criteria

- Stakeholder feedback is captured.
- Sprint outcome is transparent.
- Retrospective observations distinguish symptoms from system causes.
- At least one improvement experiment has a measurable hypothesis.
- Improvement work is visible in a backlog.
- Follow-up ownership is explicit.
