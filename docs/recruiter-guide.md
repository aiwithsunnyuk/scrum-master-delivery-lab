# Recruiter Guide

## What this repository demonstrates

This is a practical Scrum Master delivery simulation covering:

- Backlog refinement
- Sprint Planning
- Sprint execution
- Impediment management
- Dependency coordination
- Metrics
- Sprint Review
- Retrospectives
- Coaching
- Stakeholder management
- Continuous improvement

## Five-minute recruiter path

Open these files in order:

1. `README.md`
2. `docs/capability-matrix.md`
3. `docs/end-to-end-story.md`
4. `data/impediment-register.csv`
5. `docs/metrics-review.md`
6. `docs/coaching-plan.md`

## Suggested recruiter message

"I've built a practical Scrum Master delivery lab to make my Agile delivery approach visible. It complements my professional experience and certifications with concrete examples of backlog refinement, Sprint execution, impediment management, metrics, stakeholder facilitation and continuous improvement."

## What not to claim

Do not present the synthetic scenario as confidential client work or as a production system.

Use it as evidence of practical thinking and as a conversation starter around real experience.
