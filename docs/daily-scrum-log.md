# Daily Scrum Log — Sprint 02

This is a simulated delivery log demonstrating how the team inspects progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapts the Sprint Backlog.

## Day 1

**Signal:** Search filtering development started.

**Observation:** Taxonomy API contract needs confirmation.

**Action:** Engineering Lead confirms contract with upstream team.

## Day 2

**Signal:** US-008 progressing normally.

**Observation:** UX copy approved.

**Action:** No adaptation required.

## Day 3

**Signal:** US-005 technical approach validated.

**Observation:** Analytics event naming still unresolved for US-009.

**Action:** Product Owner and analytics owner scheduled a clarification discussion.

## Day 4 — Impediment emerges

**Signal:** Article-taxonomy service becomes unavailable because of an upstream production incident.

**Impact:** US-005 cannot complete its integration path.

**Scrum Master response:**

- Make the impediment visible.
- Facilitate contact with the upstream service owner.
- Help Developers inspect whether another Sprint Backlog item can advance.
- Keep the Sprint Goal as the decision filter.
- Avoid turning the Daily Scrum into a long problem-solving meeting.

## Day 5

**Signal:** Taxonomy service ETA remains uncertain.

**Adaptation:** Team advances US-008 and US-012 while maintaining the Sprint Goal.

## Day 6

**Signal:** Taxonomy service restored in test environment.

**Action:** Developers resume integration.

## Day 8

**Signal:** Filtering works in integration testing.

**Observation:** Relevance-indicator copy needs Product Owner clarification.

**Action:** PO confirms wording during refinement.

## Day 9

**Signal:** Regression testing underway.

**Risk:** Limited QA availability.

**Action:** Developers pair with QA earlier instead of waiting for a final handoff.

## Day 10

**Signal:** Sprint Goal achieved.

**Outcome:** Search filtering and empty-state experience demonstrated successfully.

## Scrum Master learning

The most useful Daily Scrum output is not a status list. It is a shared understanding of whether the team needs to adapt its plan to achieve the Sprint Goal.
