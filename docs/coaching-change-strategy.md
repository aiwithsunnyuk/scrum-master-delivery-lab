# Coaching & Change Strategy

## Stakeholder groups
- Product Owners: decision clarity, prioritisation and capacity
- Developers: autonomy, flow and sustainable delivery
- Managers: outcome-oriented leadership and reduced status interruption
- Business stakeholders: transparent feedback and clear decision paths
- Delivery leadership: systemic risks, dependencies and measurable outcomes

## Coaching pattern
`Observe → Ask → Reflect → Experiment → Inspect → Reinforce`

Coach behaviours in context. Avoid positioning Agile transformation as a compliance exercise.
