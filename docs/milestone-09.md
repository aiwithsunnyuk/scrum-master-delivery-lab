# Milestone 09 — Delivery Leadership & Transformation Lab

## Objective

Extend the Scrum Master evidence from Sprint-level facilitation into delivery leadership: release readiness, delivery risk, cross-team dependencies, Agile governance and executive decision support.

## Core model

**Sense → Frame → Facilitate → Decide → Act → Inspect → Learn**

The Scrum Master does not become the owner of every risk or decision. The role is to improve transparency, facilitate the right conversation, help establish ownership, surface systemic constraints and support evidence-based adaptation.
